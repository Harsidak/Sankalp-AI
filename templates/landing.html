Common Questions

Is my conversation private?

Yes. Your chats are stored securely in your personal account linked to your Firebase ID. We do not sell your data. Conversations are used solely to provide you with support.

Is this a replacement for therapy?

No. Sankalp is a self-help tool and a companion. It is not a licensed therapist. If you are in crisis or have a severe mental health condition, please seek professional help immediately.

Is it really free?

Yes! Our core mission is to make mental health support accessible to students. All standard chat and tracking features are completely free to use.
